Talk with Tom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Talk with Tom ("the app", package com.abubakar.tomcat) is a children's entertainment app. We respect your privacy and your child's privacy.

What the app uses

  • Microphone: used so Tom can hear speech and repeat it. Sound is processed live on the device, kept only in memory for a few seconds while Tom repeats it, then discarded. It is never saved to files or sent anywhere.
  • Camera (optional): used so Tom can copy your movement. Camera frames are analysed on the device in real time and immediately discarded. No photos or videos are taken, saved or sent.
  • Colour choice: your chosen colour for Tom is saved on the device only, so it is remembered next time.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.
  • The app does not send any data over the internet.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Text-to-speech is provided by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (such as Google's). Typed text is passed to that engine on the device so Tom can speak it.

Children

The app is suitable for children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Permissions

You can turn off microphone or camera access at any time in your phone's Settings; the app will keep working without them.
